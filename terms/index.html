STUDIO POLICIES

Terms & Conditions

1. Free Demo Class Policy

First-time students are eligible for one complimentary demo class subject to schedule availability and prior booking confirmation.

2. Studio Code of Conduct

All students and parents must maintain respectful studio decorum, wear appropriate non-marking footwear or dance shoes, and arrive 5 minutes prior to class scheduled start times.

3. Event & Choreography Bookings

Sangeeth, wedding, and corporate choreography sessions require advance schedule confirmation. Rescheduling is allowed with 24 hours prior notice.
