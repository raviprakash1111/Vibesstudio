About VIBES STUDIO | Dance & Fitness Studio in Vizag

VIBES STUDIO is a dedicated dance, fitness, and creative learning studio in Aganampudi, Visakhapatnam. We foster artistic expression, confidence, and physical wellness through structured dance classes, certified Zumba fitness, customized event choreography, creative art workshops, and academic tuitions.
